Workflow

From blueprint to walkable VR

A repeatable four-stage pipeline that turns technical drawings into immersive, real-time experiences.

STEP 01

Blueprint / 2D Layout

Interpret floor plans, dimensions, and requirements to establish the design foundation.

STEP 02

CAD Drafting & BIM

Model precise geometry and building information in AutoCAD, Revit, and Fusion 360.

STEP 03

3D Asset Creation

Sculpt materials, lighting, and detailed assets in Blender for a photoreal scene.

STEP 04

Real-Time VR Walkthrough

Assemble an interactive, walkable experience in Unreal Engine 5 for immersive presentation.
